Our Story

More than a cup —
a community.

Cannon Coffee started with a simple belief: that a great neighborhood café should feel like it belongs to its neighborhood. We're tucked into South Austin with locally roasted beans, house-made syrups, and a wall that changes with the seasons.

We partner with Austin's bakers, roasters, and makers because the best cup is one that's part of something bigger.

Locally sourced: beans roasted right here in Austin, baked goods from Moonlight Bakery and Misty Morning Bakery
Made with care: homie-made sauces, syrups, and signature drinks you won't find anywhere else
Community first: hosting local artists year-round and keeping South Austin weird, one cup at a time

"Quality coffee by authentic people — that's not a tagline, it's how we do things every single day."

— Cannon Coffee, Austin TX
